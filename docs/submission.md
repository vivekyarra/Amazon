**Name:** DONE/IRL

**Tagline:** APIs lie. Reality doesn’t.

**Problem:** Assistants remember a sentence but too often treat a reminder, delivery claim or button press as proof that the promised outcome happened.

**What we built:** A shared household reality ledger.
